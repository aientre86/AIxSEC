## Executive Summary

Enterprise automation is shifting from rigid rule-based Robotic Process Automation (RPA) towards autonomous LLM-powered agent workflows capable of reasoning, calling tools, and self-correcting.

---

## Architectural Pattern

1. **Planner**: Deconstructs high-level objectives into tasks.
2. **Tool Orchestration**: Safely invokes internal APIs, databases, and microservices.
3. **Verification Loop**: Evaluates output against structured schema before committing side effects.
4. **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)**: Escalates ambiguous decisions or high-risk operations to human operators.

---

## Key Outcomes

- Reduced cycle time on multi-step workflows.
- Improved adaptiveness to unstructured schema or documents.
- Continuous telemetry and traceable audit trails.
